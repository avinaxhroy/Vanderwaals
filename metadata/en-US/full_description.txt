WALLPAPERS THAT LEARN YOUR STYLE

Vanderwaals learns your visual style on-device and delivers wallpapers matched to your taste. No endless scrolling required.

HOW IT WORKS

• Auto Mode: Start fresh and let the app learn as you like or hide wallpapers
• Personalize Mode: Pick a starting style to match right away
• Flexible Schedules: Rotate wallpapers on screen unlock, hourly, daily, or whenever you choose
• Apply Anywhere: Set wallpapers for your lock screen, home screen, or both

ON-DEVICE PERSONALIZATION

MobileNetV4 analyzes style and visual elements directly on your phone:
• Style & Mood: Evaluates visual motifs and artistic styles
• Color Harmony: Matches palettes using perceptual LAB color space
• Aesthetic Categories: Learns preferences across nature, architecture, minimalist, and dark aesthetics

PRIVACY FIRST

✓ 100% On-Device AI: Machine learning models run entirely on your phone
✓ Zero Analytics: No tracking SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics
✓ No Cloud Account: Your taste profile stays in local storage on your device
✓ Open Source (AGPL-3.0): Full transparency you can inspect and verify
✓ Minimal Network Use: Internet is used only to download wallpapers and catalog updates

CURATED COLLECTIONS

Thousands of wallpapers organized across curated catalogs:
• Photography: Landscapes, nature, and architectural photography
• Digital Art: Minimalist illustrations, geometric patterns, and aesthetic palettes
• Automatic Catalog Updates: Fresh collections added over time

MATERIAL 3 DESIGN

• Clean interface built with modern Material Design 3
• Dynamic color theming matching your current wallpaper
• Dark theme support with high-contrast tactile elements
• Wallpaper history with quick like, hide, and download actions

REQUIREMENTS

• Android 12.0 or higher
• ~50MB storage
• Internet connection for catalog downloads (matching works offline)
