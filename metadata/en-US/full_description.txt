🎨 WALLPAPERS THAT LEARN YOUR AESTHETIC

Vanderwaals uses on-device machine learning to learn your style and deliver wallpapers you'll love. No endless scrolling—just fresh wallpapers that match your vibe.

✨ HOW IT WORKS

• Auto Mode: Discover wallpapers, the app learns from your feedback
• Personalize Mode: Upload your favorite for instant matches
• Auto-change triggers: Every unlock, hourly, daily, or manual
• Apply to lock screen, home screen, or both

🤖 PERSONALIZATION

Machine learning analyzes your aesthetic through:
• Deep visual features (artistic style & mood via MobileNetV4)
• Color matching (LAB color space for accurate palettes)
• Category learning (minimal, nature, abstract, dark themes, etc.)

The more you use it, the better it predicts what you'll love.

📚 MASSIVE CURATED LIBRARY

Thousands of high-quality wallpapers from:
• GitHub: Aesthetic designs, anime, color schemes
• Bing: Professional UHD photography & landscapes
• Weekly auto-updates with fresh content
