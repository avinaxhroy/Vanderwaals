🤖 PERSONALIZATION

Vanderwaals uses on-device machine learning to understand your aesthetic:

• Deep Visual Features (70%)
  MobileNetV4 neural network analyzes artistic style, composition, and mood

• Color Matching (20%)
  LAB color space for perceptually accurate palette matching

• Category Learning (10%)
  Learns your preferences (minimal, nature, abstract, dark themes, etc.)

The more you use it, the better it gets at predicting what you'll love.

🧠 ADAPTIVE LEARNING

• Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
  Smoothly integrates new preferences without forgetting old ones

• Implicit Feedback
  Learns from wallpaper duration (quick changes = dislike, long = like)

• Smart Exploration
  Occasionally suggests new styles to keep things fresh

• Progressive Improvement
  Balances your original taste with newly discovered preferences
