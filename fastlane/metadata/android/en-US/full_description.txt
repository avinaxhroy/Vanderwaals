WALLPAPERS THAT LEARN YOUR STYLE

Vanderwaals learns your visual style on-device and delivers wallpapers matched to your taste. No endless scrolling required.

HOW IT WORKS

Pick Your Starting Point
• Auto Mode: Start fresh and discover new wallpapers
• Personalize Mode: Pick a reference image or aesthetic preset to match immediately

Rate Wallpapers
• Tap Like on wallpapers you enjoy
• Tap Hide on wallpapers that don't match your taste
• The on-device recommendation model adapts with every rating

Automatic Rotations
• Update on screen unlock (with battery cooldown)
• Update on regular schedules (hourly, daily, or custom times)
• Browse and apply manually anytime

ON-DEVICE PERSONALIZATION

Vanderwaals runs a local MobileNetV4 model directly on your device:
• Style & Composition: Analyzes artistic style and visual complexity
• Color Palette: Matches colors using perceptually uniform LAB color space
• Category Preferences: Balances preferences across minimalist, dark, nature, and abstract collections

PRIVACY FIRST

Built privacy-first from day one:
✓ 100% On-Device AI: Machine learning runs entirely on your phone
✓ Zero Analytics: No tracking SDKs, Firebase, or external telemetry
✓ No Cloud Account: Preference vectors and history never leave your device
✓ Open Source (AGPL-3.0): Inspect and verify the code yourself
✓ Network calls only for: Downloading wallpaper assets and catalog updates

CURATED COLLECTIONS

Thousands of wallpapers across curated sources:
• Curated Collections: Minimalist graphics, digital art, and aesthetic themes
• Bing Photography: High-resolution landscapes and architecture
• Automatic Catalog Updates: Fresh wallpapers added regularly

MATERIAL 3 DESIGN

• Modern interface built with Material Design 3
• Dynamic color support matching your active wallpaper
• Dark theme with high-contrast tactile elements
• Detailed history timeline with quick like, hide, and save actions

REQUIREMENTS

• Android 12.0 or higher
• ~50MB storage
• Internet connection for catalog downloads (matching works offline)
